About Thamizhum Naanum
An exclusive Thamizh Classical repertoire by Sangita Kalanidhi Shri Sanjay Subrahmanyan, celebrating the musical soul of Chennai.
A Cherished Tradition
The kutcheri-goers’ favourite, Thamizhum Naanum is an exclusive Thamizh Classical repertoire by Sangita Kalanidhi Shri Sanjay Subrahmanyan. A concert that has won hearts across the globe, it continues to celebrate the musical soul of Chennai.
The Ninth Ahaaa! Awaits.
Since 2018, Thamizhum Naanum has given rasikas countless reasons to say Ahaaa! A familiar kriti sung in a new way. An unexpected musical turn. A composition that stays with you long after the concert ends.
Now, as the concert returns for its 9th edition, Sanjay Subrahmanyan brings another evening of Thamizh music, fresh discoveries and surprises to look forward to.
Because with Sanjay, there’s always another Ahaaa! waiting to happen.
Thamizh, Rediscovered!
Sanjay Subrahmanyan returns with a Tamil repertoire that rewards curiosity. Beloved classics meet rarely heard gems, each finding fresh expression through his distinctive artistry. A familiar phrase takes an unexpected turn. A forgotten composition becomes a new favourite. And rasikas discover, once again, why every performance deserves to be experienced live.
