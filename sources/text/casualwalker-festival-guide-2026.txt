The Chennai Music Festival 2026-2027, celebrated as the Margazhi Music and Dance Festival, stands as Asia’s largest classical music festival and a UNESCO-recognized cultural phenomenon showcasing South India’s artistic legacy. Running annually from mid-December 2026 through mid-January 2027, this internationally acclaimed December Season Chennai features over 1,500 concerts across 300+ venues, presenting Carnatic music concerts, Bharatanatyam dance performances, classical drama, and traditional art exhibitions by prestigious Chennai Sabhas.
