When Chennai’s December Festival fills the city with music, a few artists become part of its rhythm. Smt. Jayalakshmi Sekhar, the renowned veena exponent, returns to the Margazhi stage this year with six concerts across the city.
A life shaped by music
Her journey began with the violin at the age of six. By ten, the veena had claimed her heart. She spent ten devoted years learning from Sri Pudukottai Jayarama Iyer in Chennai, mastering the instrument’s finest nuances.
Her musical world grew richer through other mentors. Through her husband, TV Sekhar, a nephew of Sangeetha Kalanidhi Padmabhushan Dr. Sripada Pinakapani, she came under the maestro’s influence and wove his distinctive style into her own playing. She also treasures having learnt the varnams and thillanas of violin maestro Lalgudi Jayaraman.
Performer, teacher, bridge between cultures
Renowned musician Smt. Jayalakshmi Sekhar has graced stages with the veena, violin and flute, and her artistry has travelled to Malaysia, Singapore, Australia, Mauritius, Estonia, Germany and the United States. She has also accompanied eminent artists on their global tours.

As a teacher, she has inspired disciples across the world. She served as Associate Professor at the Tamil Nadu Music and Fine Arts University. From 2007 to 2010 she taught children at the Cleveland Festival through the Sustaining Sampradaya initiative, alongside her solo performances. Her deep knowledge of nattuvangam has made her a sought-after collaborator for Bharatanatyam artistes in Chennai and abroad.
