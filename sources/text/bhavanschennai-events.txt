Neela Subramanian Endowment
Born to Srimathi Lakshmi Ammal and Sri Tyagaraman on 25th
June, 1925, Neela Subramanian was a strong, brave and determined
woman. She devoted her life to the pursuit of music and the service
of God.
An excellent Veena player, she was honoured to play the
instrument several times for the Kanchi Paramacharya Sri
Chandrasekharendra Saraswathi. In fact, it was tradition for the
Paramacharya to ask her to play the veena after his final pooja at the
Matham as well as in many of the transit camps he was at.
Her passion for music was channelised through teaching of Veena.
A strict disciplinarian, she sought the same flawless perfection from
her students that she herself exhibited.
Her gift for music comes as no surprise. Her father, late Sri
Tyagaraman, was the grandson of Umayalpuram Sri Krishna
Bhagavathar; the direct disciple of the saint composer Sri
Thyagaraja Swamigal. She thus belongs to the Shishya Parampara
of Tyagaraja and pursued the art of Carnatic music with great
reverence and zeal.
Neela Subramanian always had a deep devotion to the Vedas and
Hindu sastras and believed ardently in their wisdom and relevance.
She sought to contribute her mite by arranging Veda Parayanam
periodically in her house at Nandanam. She has also given a
handsome donation to the Kanchi Matham for the preservation and
protection of Vedas.
In keeping with her last wish and desire to encourage the spread of
Vedic wisdom, an endowment has been created under Bharatiya
Vidya Bhavan, Chennai Kendra.
Her spirit and passion will live on as we commemorate her life.
