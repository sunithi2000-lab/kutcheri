A Festival of Music, Dance and Drama
Welcome to Brahma Gana Sabha, one of the oldest Sabha’s in Chennai. Founded in 1969 by Sri.Sethuraman, Sri. M.S.Muthu and M.Krishnamuthi, BGS has grown into a huge brand sought after by artists & art connoisseurs alike.
With Sri. Nalli Kuppuswami Chettiar at the helm as it’s President and Sri. S.Ravichandran & Sri. N.Balasubramanian as the secretaries, BGS has no dearth for events promoting Classical Dance, Music and Theatre in Chennai all year round.
Stay updated with upcoming programs at Brahma Gana Sabha.
